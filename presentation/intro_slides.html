FORGE-VISION · INTRODUCTION
What it is

A real-time AI training coach
that never blinks

  • Watches a worker perform a task through an ordinary camera.
  • Compares what they do against the approved Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).
  • The moment it detects a deviation, it generates instant, plain-language guidance — what went wrong, and why.
FORGE-VISION · INTRODUCTION
The problem

Error happens during execution —
feedback arrives too late

  • Much workplace error is procedural deviation at the moment of execution, not lack of knowledge.
  • Traditional training is retrospective: feedback comes hours or days later, when its teaching value has faded.
  • FORGE-Vision closes the gap — feedback in the moment, specific to the exact step.
FORGE-VISION · INTRODUCTION
How it works

A four-layer real-time pipeline

1 · Perception
OpenCV frame processing · MediaPipe body pose · YOLOv8 tool & PPE detection
2 · Recognition
MobileNetV3 + LSTM action model — ~85.8% accuracy · 62 ms per frame
3 · Intelligence
SOP deviation detector → Large Language Model writes the correction
4 · Guidance
Explainable feedback delivered to the worker in < 2 seconds
