The Problem

Error happens during execution —
feedback arrives too late

  • A large share of operational error stems from deviation from Standard Operating Procedures at the moment of execution — not from a lack of knowledge.
  • Traditional training is episodic and retrospective: train → assess → feedback, long after the error, when its instructional value has decayed.
  • The research problem is one of timing and specificity: can a system detect a procedural deviation as it happens and deliver corrective, explainable guidance immediately?
FORGE-VISION · SYSTEM WALKTHROUGH
Research Hypotheses

Three testable claims

H1  Employees trained with FORGE-Vision achieve a statistically significant reduction in task-error rates vs. traditional training (p < 0.05).
H2  The hybrid pathway (simulation → real-time) yields better skill transfer than simulation-only or real-time-only.
H3  AI-generated real-time feedback reduces task-completion time vs. post-hoc feedback.

System Architecture

A four-layer real-time pipeline

1 · Perception
OpenCV frame processing · MediaPipe pose (33 body + 42 hand landmarks) · YOLOv8 tool & PPE detection
2 · Recognition
MobileNetV3 + LSTM action recognition — 2.3M params · 85.8% accuracy · 62 ms p95 latency (edge-ready)
3 · Intelligence
SOP deviation detector → structured context → LLM (Groq Llama-3.3) generates the corrective instruction
4 · Guidance
Explainable feedback delivered to the worker in < 2 seconds — what was wrong, and why
PyTorchOpenCVMediaPipeYOLOv8 FastAPIStreamlitPostgreSQLDockerGroq LLM

Conclusion

Contribution

FORGE-Vision shows that real-time, explainable, AI-generated guidance can be delivered within a two-second budget on commodity hardware — that a hybrid simulation-to-reality pathway measurably outperforms single-modality training — and that the platform doubles as a rigorous instrument for testing those claims.
